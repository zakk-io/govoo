# Governance Traceability Chains

Govoo's data model supports two governance traceability chains end-to-end. This note assesses, for each, whether a user can actually *navigate* the chain in a few clicks in the current UI — not whether the underlying data link exists (both chains' underlying foreign keys appear to exist; the question is UI reachability).

## Chain 1: Meeting → Agenda → Resolution → Vote → Minutes

**Verdict: partially navigable, with real breaks in both directions.**

Forward navigation (Meeting → Minutes) works reasonably well for the Company Secretary / Board Administrator backend view: a meeting record has related Minutes and Board Pack records reachable via smart buttons, and the Resolutions list generally links each resolution back to its originating meeting (SEC-008 found one exception — an orphaned "Approve Consulting Services Agreement with TechHub Rwanda Ltd" resolution with no meeting link at all, a genuine audit-trail dead end for that one record). Backward and lateral navigation is weaker: on the **portal side**, a director can reach a meeting's agenda but the agenda items are not linked to the corresponding open vote on `/my/votes` in either direction (DIR-003) — a director voting on a resolution sees a bare title with no path back to the meeting, board pack, or full resolution text, which breaks the chain exactly at the point where an informed decision is supposed to happen. The Vote → Minutes link is further undermined because the activities that are supposed to drive directors to vote are misattributed to "OdooBot" rather than the real voter (SEC-004, BOA-008, AUD-006), so the "who was asked, who responded" segment of the chain is not trustworthy even where the records are technically linked. Once a meeting reaches Minutes, the record itself displays with a raw `govoo.minutes,<id>` title rather than a readable name (SEC-002, AUD-003), which doesn't break the chain mechanically but makes it hard for a human to confirm, at a glance, which meeting's minutes they have arrived at. Net: a Company Secretary assembling the full story of one decision (agenda item → resolution → how each director voted → minutes language) can get there, but only by manually cross-referencing multiple screens, not by clicking through; a Director or Shareholder trying to do the same from the portal cannot get there at all for the vote-to-resolution-text leg. Relevant findings: SEC-002, SEC-004, SEC-008, DIR-001, DIR-003, BOA-001, BOA-008, AUD-002, AUD-003, AUD-006.

## Chain 2: Register Entry → Statutory Filing

**Verdict: navigable for locating the record, but the resulting data cannot be trusted at face value.**

The Statutory Registers menu (Directors, Members, Beneficial Owners, Charges) and the Compliance Instances list are both directly reachable in 2 clicks from any backend role's home, and a Board Administrator or Company Secretary can open a Register of Charges entry or a Compliance Instance and see its filing status (Late/Upcoming/Filed/Waived) without needing to traverse multiple screens — structurally this chain is short and direct, better than Chain 1. However, the audit evidence quality at the end of this chain is seriously compromised: the Audit Ledger — the one screen whose job is to show the change history behind each register entry — displays only raw technical model names instead of register labels (BOA-005) and attributes 100% of 26 sampled historical changes to "OdooBot" rather than any real user (BOA-006), so while a user can *navigate to* the audit trail in a couple of clicks, what they find there cannot answer "who changed this register entry and when" for a single real person. Separately, a live QA/test-fixture record ("[LIVE-TEST #236] Reminder Fixture Partner") was found sitting inside the real Register of Charges (BOA-004), meaning the register a user navigates to is not even a clean, authoritative dataset at the moment of this audit. The Governance Settings screen that defines the statutory deadlines driving this chain's own compliance flags (Charge Registration, Beneficial Owner Declaration, Share Certificate/Transfer deadlines) currently shows all four thresholds at 0 days with no on-screen explanation that they are unconfirmed placeholders (SEC-003, BOA-003) — so even when a user successfully navigates to a Compliance Instance's Late/Upcoming status, they cannot be confident the underlying deadline math is real. Relevant findings: BOA-003, BOA-004, BOA-005, BOA-006, SEC-003, SEC-006.

## Risk / Control / Finding / Action-Plan traceability: not applicable

Govoo has no Risk, Controls, or Attestation module. There is no risk register, control library, test-of-control, finding, or remediation/action-plan model anywhere in the menus observed by any of the five roles audited (Company Secretary, Board Administrator, Director, Shareholder, Auditor). GRC rubric items that assume a Risk-Control-Test-Finding traceability chain (risk heat maps, KRI trend lines, control-to-finding linkage) are stated here plainly as **not applicable to this product** and were not scored anywhere in this audit, consistent with the pre-agreed scope cut for this first pass.
